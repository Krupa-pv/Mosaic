MAPLE GROVE CARE CENTER
Resident Care Plan — Room 214

Resident:      Margaret Chen
Prepared by:   R. Alvarez, RN

MOBILITY AND SAFETY
Margaret ambulates with a walker and has increased fall risk. Requires a
clear path and seated activities where possible.

SENSORY
Mild hearing impairment. Prefers rooms without competing background
noise. Vision within normal range.

COGNITION
Mild cognitive impairment. Follows conversation well one to one and in
small groups; struggles to keep up in larger rooms.

ENERGY AND ROUTINE
Performs best in the morning and becomes fatigued later in the
afternoon. Morning programming is strongly preferred.

SOCIAL AND INTERESTS
Margaret enjoys gardening and previously participated in cooking groups.
She kept an allotment for decades. Quiet until she knows someone, then
conversational. Prefers small groups to large gatherings.

NOTES
Daughter Anne visits on Sundays. Three grandchildren, whom she talks
about often.
