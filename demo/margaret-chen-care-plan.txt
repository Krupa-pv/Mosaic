Resident Care Plan — Room 214

Resident:      Margaret Chen
Reviewed:      2 September 2026
Prepared by:   R. Alvarez, RN

MOBILITY AND SAFETY
Margaret ambulates with a walker and has increased fall risk. She needs a
clear path to her seat and should be offered seated activities wherever
possible.

SENSORY
Mild hearing impairment on the left. She follows conversation well in a
quiet room but loses the thread where there is competing background
noise. Vision is within normal range with reading glasses.

COGNITION
Mild cognitive impairment. She follows conversation comfortably one to
one and in small groups, and struggles to keep up in a larger room.

ENERGY AND ROUTINE
Margaret performs best in the morning and becomes fatigued later in the
afternoon. Morning programming is strongly preferred.

INTERESTS OBSERVED ON THE UNIT
She enjoys gardening and previously participated in cooking groups on
the unit.

NOTES
No dietary restrictions. Needs no supervision for meals.
