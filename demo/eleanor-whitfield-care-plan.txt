MOBILITY AND SAFETY
Eleanor ambulates independently indoors but uses a cane for any distance
beyond the corridor. Steady on her feet; no falls recorded in the past
twelve months. Low fall risk.

SENSORY
Vision is corrected with reading glasses and within normal range.
Hearing is within normal range. No aids required.

COGNITION
Cognition intact. Oriented to person, place and time. Manages her own
medication schedule with a weekly check.

ENERGY AND ROUTINE
Eleanor is most alert in the afternoon and prefers not to be disturbed
before nine in the morning. She tires by early evening.

SOCIAL AND INTERESTS
Retired botany teacher. She kept a greenhouse for many years and speaks
often about gardening and about growing orchids in particular. She reads
widely and has asked twice whether the home has a book group. She enjoys
cooking programmes on television.

She describes herself as happier in small groups than large ones, and
says she finds a crowded room tiring rather than unpleasant.

NOTES

weekends. Eleanor mentioned she would like to meet people who garden.
