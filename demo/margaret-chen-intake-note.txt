MAPLE GROVE CARE CENTER
Social Intake Note — Room 214

Resident:  Margaret Chen
Taken by:  D. Whitmore, Life Enrichment

Margaret grew up in Sacramento, California, and moved here in the spring
to be nearer her daughter.

She taught fourth grade for thirty-one years and still talks about her
students. She kept an allotment for decades and grew tomatoes and
dahlias, and she lights up talking about gardening.

She also loves jazz — she saw Ella Fitzgerald live twice — and watches
cooking shows most afternoons.

Her daughter Anne visits, and she has three grandchildren she mentions
constantly.

Every morning she likes to read the paper before breakfast and is slow
to get going before that.

Margaret is quiet and fairly reserved until she knows someone, and then
she is very conversational. She is happier in small groups and doesn't
like huge gatherings — she says a crowded room wears her out.
