# Demo files

Care plans to upload during the demo. Drag either onto the **Care plan**
panel on a resident's Profile tab, or use the Upload button.

| File | Resident | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `eleanor-whitfield-care-plan.txt` | Eleanor Whitfield, room 118 | New admission, nothing on file. The full build-from-scratch path. |
| `margaret-chen-care-plan.txt` | Margaret Chen, room 214 | The §6 demo pair. Her profile is deliberately empty so this can be built live. |

**Who has no profile on file:** Margaret and Eleanor. Everyone else is
already profiled, so they show their profile immediately rather than
asking you to rebuild it.

What to expect after uploading Eleanor's:

1. Her profile fills in — gardening, orchids, reading, cooking; cane,
   low fall risk, intact cognition; afternoons, small groups.
2. The Companion tab stops refusing and ranks the floor against her.
3. Accepting a pairing puts her on that activity's roster, and she moves
   out of the isolated ring on **Your floor**.
