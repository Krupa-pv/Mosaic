### Margaret, end to end

Upload the **care plan** first, then the **intake note**. Between them
they fill every field:

| From the care plan | From the intake note |
|---|---|
| Mobility · Walker | Interests · gardening, cooking, jazz, family |
| Fall risk · High | Group size · Small |
| Hearing · Mild | Conversation · Quiet |
| Vision · Normal | Social energy · reserved |
| Cognition · Mild impairment | From · Sacramento, California |
| Best time · Morning | Work · taught fourth grade for 31 years |
| Interests · gardening, cooking | Family · daughter Anne, three grandchildren |
| | Routines · reads the paper before breakfast |

Then: **Companion** ranks the floor and puts Helen first, with Indoor
Garden Circle on Wednesday morning. **Schedule** shows both weeks side
by side. Accepting puts them on the roster, and she moves in from the
edge of the connection map on **Your floor** — where she also becomes
placeable in next week's plan.

What to expect after uploading Eleanor's:

1. Her profile fills in — gardening, orchids, reading, cooking; cane,
   low fall risk, intact cognition; afternoons, small groups.
2. The Companion tab stops refusing and ranks the floor against her.
3. Accepting a pairing puts her on that activity's roster, and she moves
   out of the isolated ring on **Your floor**.
